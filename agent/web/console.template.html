What is live, and what is simulated

  • Live: the agent's plan and every tool call behind this page. The confound gate, the marker-eligibility gate, the scope routing and the refusal to lower an urgency are all enforced in the server, not in a prompt.
  • Live: this page is generated from the same store the agent writes to, so it cannot show something the agent did not do.
  • Live: every caption and every line of both appointment summaries is written by the agent, and checked against the consultation before it is shown. A dose, an interval or a medicine the doctor did not say is refused.
  • Simulated: the phone call. One function returns a fixed transcript. Telephony replaces its body and nothing else.
  • Simulated: the consultation is replayed from a stored transcript rather than recognised live. Streaming recognition replaces the one function that hands out segments; nothing downstream of it changes.
  • Simulated: no speech recognition, synthesis or dialect model is wired up. The Tier 2 marker eligibility shown here is asserted by the seed, not measured.
  • Invented: the elder, her family, her helper, her medication list, and every day of history on this page.
